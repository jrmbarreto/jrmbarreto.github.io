n8n Job Search

A personal automation run by João Barreto

n8n Job Search is a private workflow that runs on my own computer. Once a day it collects job postings for my own job search: it reads the LinkedIn job alert emails in my Gmail, checks public company job boards, adds new roles to my job tracker in Google Sheets and saves the posting text to a folder in my Google Drive.

The app is used only by me, with my own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.
