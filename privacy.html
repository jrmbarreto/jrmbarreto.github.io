Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

n8n Job Search is a personal automation used only by its owner, João Barreto, with his own Google account. It runs on his own computer through n8n.

What Google data it uses

  • Gmail: reads job alert emails from LinkedIn to collect job titles, companies and links. It does not send, delete or share email.
  • Google Sheets: reads and adds rows in the owner's job tracker spreadsheet.
  • Google Drive: creates text files with job posting content in one folder of the owner's Drive.

How the data is handled

All data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models. No data from any other person's Google account is accessed.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page under Security, Third party apps and services.
